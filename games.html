Forth for TIC-80

A port of the Forth language (based on pforth) as a scripting language for TIC-80, the fantasy console for making, playing and sharing tiny retro games. Adds Forth to the list of available cartridge languages, alongside Lua, JavaScript, Ruby, and others.

https://github.com/luginf/TIC-80
