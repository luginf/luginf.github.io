Forth for TIC-80

A port of the Forth language (based on pforth) as a scripting language for TIC-80, the fantasy console for making, playing and sharing tiny retro games. Adds Forth to the list of available cartridge languages, alongside Lua, JavaScript, Ruby, and others.

https://github.com/luginf/TIC-80

Halebarde et Gonfanon

A turn-based strategy game in pygame, adapted from the board game of the same name. Two kingdoms face each other on a bounded battlefield: mail-clad soldiers, archers, and scouts able to infiltrate the enemy camp. Two players locally or over the network, or against an AI at three levels, with three graphical themes.

github.com/luginf/halebarde_et_gonfanon

Tic-tac-toe for Uxn

Two tic-tac-toe games for Hundredrabbits' Varvara/Uxn virtual machine: a 3x3 grid and a 5x5 grid where you need four in a row. Playable with the mouse or the keyboard.

github.com/luginf/uxneries
