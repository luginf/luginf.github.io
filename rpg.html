Sparkwyrd

A Tcl/Tk reimplementation of the Inspiration Pad Pro random text generator, compatible with .ipt files (weighted tables, dice expressions, variables, filters). Useful for encounter generators, NPC creators, treasure tables, or story seeds for tabletop RPGs.

https://github.com/luginf/Sparkwyrd

Escapades

A small text-mode roguelike in Lua, made for coffee breaks and endlessly replayable: a generated surface world (plains, forests, a village), caves and a multi-level tower, all procedural. Field of view, combat using the ESCAPADES tabletop RPG system, scriptable quests, bilingual French and English. CP437 rendering, playable in a terminal as well as on the Linux console. The introductory adventure is The Whispering Tower.

github.com/luginf/escapades-roguelike

Yi Jing

A small Tcl/Tk desktop app to identify an I Ching hexagram by toggling its six yin/yang lines with a click.

github.com/luginf/hexagrams
