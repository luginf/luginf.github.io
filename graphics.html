Vector World

A retro, flat-shaded low-poly 3D world, in the style of 16-bit demos: procedural mountains, Bayer dithering, wireframe, a day/night cycle, CRT effects (scanlines, vignette) and a heightmap editor. Built with Three.js, Vite and simplex-noise.

Try it online - github.com/luginf/3D_retro
