wmdcalc and wmdockapps

wmdcalc is a small calculator dock app for Window Maker, with three input modes: Forth (reverse Polish notation), Lisp (s-expressions) and classic. A popup window gives a larger keypad, and keyboard input and the clipboard are supported. It is built on my fork of wmdockapps, a C++ library for writing themed dock apps (the picture shows the apps shipped with it).
