Di-111 synth

A VST3 plugin that emulates the Roland D-110 multitimbral sound module. Runs the real Roland firmware - menus, display, patch/timbre editors, all sixteen front-panel buttons - against an emulated LA32 engine (munt). Requires your own Roland ROM dumps (not included, copyrighted).

github.com/luginf/Di-111-synth

JiV-881 synth

An unofficial emulator of the Roland JV-880 rompler (a 1U rack unit from 1992), based on Nuked-SC55 and jv880_juce. Available as VST3, AU, LV2 and CLAP plugins, as a standalone app (macOS, Windows, Linux/AppImage) and as an Android app. Requires your own Roland ROM dumps (not included).

github.com/luginf/JiV-881-synth

Hexacorde

An oracular step sequencer: six I Ching hexagrams sit on a grid (6x6 by default, from 3x3 to 12x12). Each line of a hexagram is a note, sent to another hexagram which echoes it in turn. The piece grows out of how the hexagrams are laid out. A single HTML page in plain JavaScript, no dependencies and no server, with four themes. Also available as a VCV Rack 2 module.

Try it online - github.com/luginf/hexacorde - VCV Rack module

Nonet Sequencer

A plain 9-track (extendable to 16) standalone MIDI sequencer: it has no sound engine of its own and drives an external synth or a DAW over MIDI ports. Three views of the same drawer: a mouse-driven classic grid, a "retro" view (a small text LCD and buttons, styled after the Roland D-20's sequencer, fully keyboard-driven) and a piano roll. Tracks default to a D-110-style channel map.

github.com/luginf/nonet-sequencer

abcAmi

A random music generator in ABC notation format, written in Tcl/Tk. You set key, scale, time signature, number of bars, rests and note-length distribution; the algorithm combines a Markov chain, hierarchical rhythm, global forms (AABA), V-I cadences and variations on repeats. Direct playback through abcmidi and timidity or fluidsynth.

github.com/luginf/abcAmi

MIDI to Furnace

A MIDI exporter for Furnace Tracker (.fur files), in pure Tcl/Tk with no dependencies. Settings for rhythmic resolution, transposition, velocity in the volume column, the number of YM2612 chips and the mapping from MIDI channels to Furnace channels.

github.com/luginf/export_midi_to_furnace

MIDI banks for Casio CDP-S350 / CDP-S360

The complete 700-voice list (18 categories) of the Casio CDP-S350/CDP-S360, converted from MusE's proprietary format (.idf) to MIDNAM, the near-universal XML format for exchanging MIDI instrument definitions. Ready to use as-is in Ardour, Mixbus, Logic Pro and Pro Tools.

Download: .midnam (Ardour, Mixbus, Logic Pro, Pro Tools) - .middev (Logic Pro, Pro Tools) - .idf (MusE, source) - conversion script
